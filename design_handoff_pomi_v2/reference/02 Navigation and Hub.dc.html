Rules
Asks only when something is live: running, paused, a break, or an auto-start countdown. From Ready, Block finished or Stopwatch-at-zero it switches straight away.
From the stopwatch: “Switch to Pomodoro?” / “This stops the stopwatch. 04:12 and 6 laps are saved.”
On a break: “This ends the break and the block. Nothing more is saved.”
Under a minute: the body reads “Less than a minute, so nothing is saved.”
Statistics, History and Settings open on top of whatever's running and never ask. The timer keeps going underneath.
Auto-start screens keep only Settings, as before, since they want a decision.
