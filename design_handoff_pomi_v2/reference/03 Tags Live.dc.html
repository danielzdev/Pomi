Editing a tag
Press and hold any tag — on now, often used, or the A–Z list — and the system menu appears: Edit and Delete. Edit brings the keyboard up in place. Delete asks once, then removes the tag and leaves its logged time in history as untagged. Renaming never cuts the timeline.
