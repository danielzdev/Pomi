The settings screen, working
Every control here is real: duration values can be typed to the minute (tap one) or nudged with ±, and the block preview re-does its arithmetic as you change them, toggles hold state, Manage tags and Alert tone push their own screens, tags can be renamed, recoloured, deleted one at a time or in bulk via Select, and Confirm before deleting actually governs whether the delete dialog appears. Nothing outside Settings exists yet — History and Statistics come next, and neither needs a setting.

What the tag settings decide
Manage tags is the library's only home — the picker sheet creates and long-press-edits, but the full list, with its logged totals, lives here. Rename and recolour are instant and never cut the timeline; delete leaves the logged time in History as untagged.
Carry tags into the next session decides whether a block is one long tagged stretch or a fresh choice each pomodoro. Confirm before deleting governs both the single-tag delete and the multi-select delete on the Tags screen.
