What's live
D / W / M / Y switches the period; the arrows in the dark card step back and forward through time.
Streak card opens the detail screen — longest streak, best day, run history, and the threshold stepper.
Year bars jump to that month.
Sessions / Blocks swaps the four reliability stats; tapping any stat opens its breakdown sheet.
Tags open a sheet; All tags opens the full list, ending with an Untagged row (untagged stretches + time from deleted tags). Long names truncate.
Before first use. History starts May 28, 2025: earlier days are faint outlines, not missed, never counted in "N of M days", and the back arrow stops there (try Y → last year).
Custom lengths. Finished / early is judged per block against the setup it started with, so mixed 25 m and 50 m history reads correctly.
Counted at recomputes every past streak when changed — deliberate.
First launch tweak: zero sessions. Today is the only day, earlier cells are faint, arrows are locked, and the stats become one card with a start button.
Weeks start on Monday, always — no setting.
Use the Stress test tweak for 5-digit sessions, 1 000+ h and a 1 000-day streak.
