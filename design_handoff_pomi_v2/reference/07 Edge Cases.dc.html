Rules at large text
Timer digits don't scale with text size. They're display type, and 56 px on a small phone still reads at arm's length.
Buttons grow in height, not width, and never go below 44 px. End and Pause stay side by side up to 160%, then stack with Pause on top.
Statistics numbers already shrink when they get long. At large text sizes the two-column stat grid becomes one column.
Settings rows wrap the hint text under the label, and the switches stay pinned to the right edge.
Tag sheet chips already wrap. Long tag names cut off with an ellipsis after one line.
